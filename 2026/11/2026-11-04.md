# 4 November 2026

The main release was a playbook for improving a personal speech-to-text model from its own transcript history, published as a repo and a blog post. Two new repos covered bulk LLM work and a format for handing research between agents, and several plugins were renamed or merged, with references updated across the marketplace.

## Speech and ASR

- **[ASR Recursive Improvement Loop](https://github.com/danielrosehill/ASR-Recursive-Improvement-Loop) (new):** a playbook and Claude Code skill for improving a personal ASR model from everyday use, released as [v1.0](https://github.com/danielrosehill/ASR-Recursive-Improvement-Loop/releases/tag/v1.0). [Blog write-up](https://blog.danielrosehill.com/posts/improve-personal-asr-model-transcript-history-fine-tuning-2o8hdi/).
- **[Fritter](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/Fritter):** speaker-adapted Parakeet model updated on Hugging Face, along with the [Tech-Sentences](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Tech-Sentences) single-speaker dataset.

## Plugins and agent tooling

- **[Batch Inference](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Batch-Inference) (new):** agent skills and scripts for bulk LLM jobs, covering model choice, route and real cost on OpenRouter batch and sync.
- **[Research Pack](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Research-Pack) (new):** a standard format for packaging research findings so one agent can hand them to the next stage of a pipeline.
- **[Tech Comms Diagrams](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Tech-Comms-Diagrams):** renamed from nano-tech-diagrams and overhauled as 2.0.0 (Mermaid, fal and Replicate, verification), then 2.1.0 with pluggable backends and a single image-to-image skill.
- **[RTL Skills](https://github.com/danielrosehill/RTL-Skills):** renamed, absorbing the rtl-email and hebrew-correspondence plugins.

## Evals

- **[AI-Eval](https://github.com/danielrosehill/AI-Eval):** renamed from eval-runner and merged with eval-authoring, adding a model-selection path, then a start-eval wrapper and eval permutation skills in v0.8.0.
- **[Tech Graphics Benchmark](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Tech-Graphics-Benchmark):** recorded an agent read of the informational and whiteboard rounds.

## Maintenance

- References to the renamed plugins updated across 10 repos, including [Claude-Marketplace-Guide-Plugin](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Marketplace-Guide-Plugin) and [ChatGPT-Plugins](https://github.com/danielrosehill/ChatGPT-Plugins).
- Image and banner skills moved to fal-ai in [Claude-Image-Production-Plugin](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Image-Production-Plugin) and [Claude-Repo-To-Content-Plugin](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Repo-To-Content-Plugin).
