# 30 April 2026

A large set of Claude Code plugins covered Linux desktop control, from KDE Plasma and OBS to CUPS printing, NFC tags, GPG, GIMP and clipboard scripting. Other plugins handled data work such as synthetic datasets, taxonomies, document conversion and diagram generation, alongside air quality, model research, digital evidence and licence tooling.
