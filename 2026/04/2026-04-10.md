# 10 April 2026

Agent plugins and templates for research workspaces and deliberation systems, plus an index of LLM Council projects. Geopolitical forecasting tools cover Iran and Israel conflict scenarios. Other repositories include a Google Workspace MCP server and MCP deployment notes.
