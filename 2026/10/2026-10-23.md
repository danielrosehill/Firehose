# 23 October 2026

Two agent plugins for Ubuntu cover machine administration and host security. The first handles health, services, logs, packages, storage, networking, hardware, Docker and KDE on desktops or servers, locally or over SSH. The second covers firewall rules, antivirus and rootkit scanning, and security posture audits.
