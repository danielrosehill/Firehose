# 20 October 2026

Android app work covered a thin fork template with weekly upstream merges and signed releases, a public F-Droid repository, and an offline family first-aid app for Israel. Other repositories hid encrypted messages in spoken sentences. Datasets graded Israel-Palestine framing outputs and labelled unsolicited outreach emails.

## GitHub Repositories

4 repos created

- **[Android Thin Fork Autosync](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Android-Thin-Fork-Autosync)** (GitHub, Tool): Template for a thin downstream fork of an active Android app: narrow hooks, weekly auto-merge of upstream releases, seam checks, signed release APKs via GitHub Actions
- **[F Droid Public Repo](https://github.com/danielrosehill/F-Droid-Public-Repo)** (GitHub, Tool): Public F-Droid repo for Daniel Rosehill's Android apps, served from Cloudflare R2 at apps.bydanielrosehill.com
- **[Family First Aid Android](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Family-First-Aid-Android)** (GitHub, Tool): Offline Android first-aid app for families in Israel (MDA / Hatzalah): adult + child protocols, interactive decision flows, spaced review. Not medical advice.
- **[LLM Spoken Steganography](https://github.com/danielrosehill/LLM-Spoken-Steganography)** (GitHub, Tool): LLM hides an encrypted message in the sentence openers of a mundane podcast that survives TTS -> air -> STT. Grew out of My Weird Prompts #6141.

## Datasets

2 datasets created

- **[Israel Palestine Framing Eval Runs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Israel-Palestine-Framing-Eval-Runs)** (Hugging Face, Evaluation dataset): Model outputs and grades from runs of Israel-Palestine-Framing-Eval. The prompts are a separate dataset, danielrosehill/Israel-Palestine-Framing-Eval.
- **[Pseudo Personalised Outreach](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/pseudo-personalised-outreach)** (Hugging Face, Training dataset): Real unsolicited emails sent to one person, labelled genuine or pseudo_personalised, with every sender identity replaced by a plausible fake.
