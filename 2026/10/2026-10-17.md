# 17 October 2026

Two audio datasets cover read-aloud English speech: one of plain everyday sentences with homophone pairs, and a small hand-picked set of real voice notes.

## Datasets

2 datasets created

- **[Annotated Voice Notes](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Annotated-Voice-Notes)** (Hugging Face, Audio dataset): A small, hand-picked set of real voice notes. It is kept small on purpose.
- **[General English Sentences](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/General-English-Sentences)** (Hugging Face, Audio dataset): Plain, everyday English sentences (emails, messages, general prose and homophone pairs) read aloud by one speaker, with no Hebrew or Yiddish words, Israeli names or tech vocabulary.
