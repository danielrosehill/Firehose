# 18 October 2026

Two tools cover automated political decision making and long term archival storage. One is a constrained model that votes for, against or abstains on parliamentary motions with a stated reason for a declared constituency. The other is a self hosted web interface that retrieves archives from cloud storage and burns them to M-DISC BD-R discs.
