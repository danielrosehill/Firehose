# 15 October 2026

Indexes of English-Hebrew code-switching work and of Hugging Face releases, plus a directory of AI activity in Jerusalem, sit alongside an agent plugin for planning custom small models. New datasets cover household inventory specification lines and voice-note transcripts, and fine-tuned models classify Hebrew words in Latin letters, voice-note intent, and inventory specification attributes.
