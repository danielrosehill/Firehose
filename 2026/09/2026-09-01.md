# 1 September 2026

Claude Code tooling for handling email exports and Timemark camera files covered agent plugins. Jerusalem work included field photography of a postering campaign with shot coordinates, an index of city projects, and a training dataset of geotagged street photos with poster bounding boxes, plus a YOLO fine-tune that identifies poster designs.

## GitHub Repositories

4 repos created

- **[Claude Eml Toolkit](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Eml-Toolkit)** (GitHub, Tool): Claude Code plugin for turning .eml email exports into artefacts you can actually use — a threaded PDF of a correspondence chain, extracted attachments, Markdown for another agent, a redacted copy...
- **[Claude Timemark Plugin](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Timemark-Plugin)** (GitHub, Agent plugin): Handle Timemark (Android stamp camera) exports: which route preserves pixels vs field values, and the join that recovers both
- **[Jerusalem Graffiti](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Graffiti)** (GitHub, Tool): Field photography of a messianic postering campaign in central Jerusalem, with the coordinates of every shot.
- **[Jerusalem Projects Index](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Projects-Index)** (GitHub, Documentation): Projects tied to Jerusalem as a place — the city, its streets, its municipal services and specific addresses within it.

## Datasets

1 dataset created

- **[Jerusalem Poster Detection](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/jerusalem-poster-detection)** (Hugging Face, Training dataset): Geotagged street photographs of a messianic postering campaign in central Jerusalem, with bounding boxes around every campaign poster in frame.

## AI Models

1 model created

- **[Jerusalem Poster Detector](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/jerusalem-poster-detector)** (Hugging Face, Other): Fine-tune of yolo11n.pt that locates specific poster designs in street photographs and names which design it found. Trained on danielrosehill/jerusalem-poster-detection.
