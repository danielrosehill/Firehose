# 6 November 2025

A pattern for AI assisted development from a single long prompt joined four fine-tuned image models on Hugging Face: two for realistic Israeli street scenes, plus character LoRAs of a plush sloth in a dinosaur costume and a handmade donkey.

## GitHub Repositories

1 repo created

- **[Claude Spec Starter](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Spec-Starter)** (GitHub, Tool): Pattern for AI assisted development starting from one (long) user prompt

## AI Models

4 models created

- **[Dinosaur Sloth](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/Dinosaur-Sloth)** (Hugging Face, Fine-tuned model): LORA for Flux created from photos of a cute sloth plushie wearing a dinosaur costume!
- **[Herman Poppleberry](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/Herman-Poppleberry)** (Hugging Face, Fine-tuned model): Character LORA created from photos of a cute donkey plushie (handcrafted in Storrs, CT!)
- **[Jerusalem Images](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Images)** (Hugging Face, Fine-tuned model): A Flux LORA for generating realistic images of Jerusalem, Israel trained on a dataset of streetscape images taken around West Jerusalem during the summer of 2025 (images: Daniel Rosehill).
- **[Tel Aviv Street Style](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/Tel-Aviv-Street-Style)** (Hugging Face, Fine-tuned model): You should use TLVSCENE to trigger the image generation.
