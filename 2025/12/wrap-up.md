## Datasets

6 datasets created

- 2025-12-03: **[Israel Photos](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Israel-Photos)** (Hugging Face, Training dataset): A collection of 369 photographs captured across Israel between 2024 and 2025, with LLM-generated captions and location annotations. The images are sourced from the photographer's Pexels gallery.
- 2025-12-09: **[ASR WPM And Background Noise Eval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/ASR-WPM-And-Background-Noise-Eval)** (Hugging Face, Audio dataset): A dataset of annotated audio recordings for evaluating how different factors affect Whisper (and other ASR/STT systems) transcription accuracy.
- 2025-12-10: **[Small STT Eval Audio Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Small-STT-Eval-Audio-Dataset)** (Hugging Face, Audio dataset): A small speech-to-text evaluation dataset containing 92 audio samples with ground truth transcriptions.
- 2025-12-10: **[STT Fine Tune Eval 101225](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/STT-Fine-Tune-Eval-101225)** (Hugging Face, Evaluation dataset): Evaluation Date: December 10, 2025
- 2025-12-12: **[Hebrew Image Eval 111225](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Hebrew-Image-Eval-111225)** (Hugging Face, Evaluation dataset): An evaluation of major text-to-image models on their ability to accurately render Hebrew text.
- 2025-12-18: **[Transcription Cleanup Trainer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Transcription-Cleanup-Trainer)** (Hugging Face, Audio dataset): A curated dataset for training speech-to-text cleanup models to achieve optimal transcript refinement.
